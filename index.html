My Story

Why I play, why I build, and why this Vault matters.

A Female in a Man's World

I Compete Because I Belong.

People often ask me why I put so much time into NBA 2K. The truth is, this story is not really about a video game. It is about finding an escape during one of the hardest times in my life, the people who helped me grow, and how a simple binder became Jackie's 2K Vault.

I work full-time and spend much of my free time caring for my mom after her stroke. There were days when life felt overwhelming, and I needed something that was just for me. NBA 2K became that escape. It gave me something to focus on, something to learn, and something to look forward to at the end of the day.

Dab always wanted me to play NBA 2K, but it was Dman who finally convinced me to give it a try because the game was free. We learned the game together from the very beginning, figuring things out one game at a time. Looking back, saying yes to that free game changed my life in ways I never expected.

The more I played, the more I wanted to improve. Every build taught me something new. Every mistake became another lesson. I started writing everything down—builds, attributes, badges, cap breakers, jump shots, animations, strengths, weaknesses, and everything else I did not want to forget.

Before I knew it, I had a binder full of notes. That binder became my playbook. Every time I learned something new, it went into the binder so I would never have to relearn it the next season.

One day I was telling Smaki about everything I had put together. I explained how much time I had invested creating my binder and everything I had learned over the year. He got genuinely excited.

“That should not just be a binder... it should be an app.”

What meant even more to me was that he wanted me to tell my story. His enthusiasm made me believe that maybe this dream could become something bigger than I had ever imagined. Without that conversation, Jackie's 2K Vault probably would not exist.

My 2K Family

One of the greatest gifts NBA 2K gave me was not just learning the game. It was the people. Dab brought me into his friend group with Mike, Quan, and Salty. They welcomed me and made me feel like I belonged. Over time, we became much more than teammates. We became family.

Each one of them helped me in a different way. They challenged me, coached me, encouraged me, laughed with me, and believed in me when I did not always believe in myself. This Vault is just as much about them as it is about me.

Dab

Dab is the person who invested the most time in helping me become a better NBA 2K player. He made builds for me, watched my game clips, showed me exactly where I was hesitating, taught me center moves, explained why they worked, and never let me off the hook. If I made the same mistake again, he made sure I learned from it.

We are still working on pump fakes. I swear I have heard “Pump fake!” a thousand times, and I am sure I will hear it another thousand.

One of our running jokes is when I ask him, “What NBA player do I play like?” At first he would say, “None... you are trash.” As I slowly improved, I finally got promoted to a recycling bin. I am still not sure I have made it to an actual NBA player yet, but at least I am making progress. 😂

Mike

Mike helped me become a better defender. If my matchup was too difficult, he would switch and take the harder player while giving me the easier assignment. While we played, he gave me pointers and taught me what I could do differently. He did not just help us win games—he helped me become a smarter defender.

Quan

Quan gave me confidence as a shooter. There were so many times I would catch the ball wide open and hesitate because I was afraid of missing. Every time, he reminded me:

“Shooters shoot... keep shooting.”

Those words became more than basketball advice. They became a mindset. He taught me to trust my shot, stop playing afraid, and believe in the work I had put in.

Dman

Dman is the reason I started playing NBA 2K. Dab wanted me to play, but Dman finally convinced me because the game was free. We learned the game together from the beginning. During one of the hardest times in my life, NBA 2K became an escape. Without Dman, I probably never would have started playing.

Smaki

Smaki played one of the biggest roles in creating Jackie's 2K Vault. I was telling him about the binder I had made to track my builds, jump shots, badges, animations, cap breakers, and everything I had learned over the year. His excitement was genuine, and he wanted me to tell my story—not just share the builds.

His enthusiasm for my idea was contagious. He believed in me before I fully believed in myself. I will always appreciate his excitement, his belief in me, and the way he encouraged me to share my story with others.

Florida

Florida reminded me that NBA 2K is supposed to be fun. Whether we were winning or losing, he brought positive energy, encouragement, laughs, and plenty of shenanigans to the party.

Chucky

Chucky always brought energy and laughter. He was patient with me, showed me little things I could do to improve, and reminded me that the friendships you build are just as important as the games you win.

Why I Built Jackie's 2K Vault

Jackie's 2K Vault started as a simple binder. Today, it is much more than that. Every build, every jump shot, every animation, every badge, every cap breaker, and every lesson represents hours of learning and the people who invested their time in helping me become a better player.

This Vault is not just my collection of builds. It is my journey. It is my thank you to everyone who helped me become the player I am today. And I hope it helps someone else become the player they want to be.

I compete because I belong.
