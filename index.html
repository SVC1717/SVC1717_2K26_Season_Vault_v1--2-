Jackie's Story

I started playing 2K after my mother's stroke while balancing work, caregiving, and family responsibilities. Basketball became a place to compete, learn, and connect.

The Vault keeps every build, lesson, jump shot, cap-breaker idea, strength, weakness, and change I wish I had made. It also gives the community a place to share without trash talk or bashing people.
